Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information submitted through the MNHS ICT/CSS enrollment form is handled.

Information collected

The form collects the learner reference number (LRN), first, middle, and last name, gender, birthday, term, class code, section, adviser, and an uploaded student photo.

Purpose and storage

The information is used to process and organize enrollment records. When submitted, the form sends it to a Google Apps Script service. Enrollment details are recorded in a Google Sheet, and the uploaded photo is saved in Google Drive with its link recorded with the enrollment.

Access and retention

Access to the Google Sheet and Drive folder depends on the permissions configured by the school. Records should be retained and access managed under the school's applicable records and privacy policies. Contact the school to ask about the retention period, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern.

Before submitting

Provide accurate information and submit only a photo you are authorized to provide for enrollment. The form requires a photo to submit an enrollment record.

Terms of Use

By using this form, you agree to the following terms:

  • Use the form only to submit an enrollment for the intended learner.
  • Provide accurate, complete information and a suitable photo you are authorized to submit.
  • Do not use the form to submit another person's information without proper authority, or to disrupt or misuse the enrollment service.
  • Submission does not by itself confirm enrollment. The school may review the information and follow up if clarification is needed.
  • The form may be unavailable or changed as needed to support enrollment operations.

For help with a submission or these terms, contact the school through its usual channels.
